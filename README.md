# 湖静旅店 · Stillwater Lodge

这是一个中文滚动恐怖故事。解压后用浏览器打开 `index.html` 即可体验。环境声默认关闭，可以在夜间值班章节开启。

## 发布到 GitHub Pages

把解压后的文件上传至 GitHub 仓库的 `main` 分支根目录。打开 **Settings → Pages**，将 **Source** 设为 **Deploy from a branch**，选择 **main** 和 **/(root)**，然后保存。部署完成后，GitHub 会在同一页面显示站点链接。[GitHub Pages 官方文档](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/configuring-a-publishing-source-for-your-github-pages-site)

## 图像素材

四张照片使用内置图像生成工具制作。下列文件位于压缩包根目录。

### `hotel.webp`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural, website hero, landscape wide 3:2. An understated luxury lakeside hotel in a misty Pacific Northwest pine forest, long low mid-century dark cedar and glass architecture, one large gabled roof reflected in perfectly still lake occupying foreground, mossy shoreline. Thick tall evergreens, overcast luminous pale gray sky. Editorial architectural photography, grain of analog film and rich muted bottle-green and soft brown hues, moody but very inviting, cultivated upscale retreat. Building spans middle, centered somewhat right, gorgeous dark reflections; no people, no logotypes, no text. Sophisticated quiet composition with distance and texture, not an illustration.

### `interior.webp`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural, horizontal 3:2 editorial boutique hotel interior photograph. Low warm amber table lamp on the right, lounge chairs, dark wood, huge panoramic glass windows to the left looking out on a perfectly still lake and foggy evergreen forest, afternoon gray light, very grounded and natural. Contemporary minimalist lodge in Pacific Northwest, tall pines and weathered materials. Fine 35mm film grain, editorial architecture photography. No people, no writing, no logos.

### `corridor.webp`

> Use case: stylized-concept treated as a photorealistic 35mm still for a found footage psychological horror website, landscape 3:2. Deep underground derelict 1970s hotel corridor, severe centralized one-point perspective, wet worn concrete flooring, black doorways on both sides, single vivid crimson emergency fixture in distant ceiling casting deep blood-red reflection, black foreground. A tiny barely visible still human-like silhouette in the distant door frame. Terrifying liminal-space atmosphere, tangible decay, moody cinematic composition, restrained red and near-black palette, analog film grain, realistic photography. No text or graphic overlays, no blood or gore.

### `presence.webp`

> Use case: stylized-concept as a widescreen 3:2 unsettling analog horror movie still. A large terrifying original uncanny supernatural face and shoulders emerge from solid pitch black, cropped around central face, wet pale grey skin, two distant unnaturally bright reflective eyes, impossibly long dark vertical mouth, messy black strands dissolving into shadows. Very muted crimson light from below picks out only sections of face, heavy shadow dominates especially corners and edges. Horror practical makeup with 35mm film grain and ultra dark dramatic lighting, composition eyes at middle height. Deep black and dark burgundy throughout, single subject, no gore, no text, no borders. Face distinctly visible in center with subtle deep blacks elsewhere.
